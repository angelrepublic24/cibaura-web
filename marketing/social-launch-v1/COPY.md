# Textos de publicación

Textos públicos en inglés. La marca Cibaura sigue siendo provisional. Las mismas leyendas sirven para feed e historias; en historias usar el CTA como texto breve, sin tapar las condiciones de la imagen. No añadir enlaces hasta conocer el dominio definitivo.

## Pre-lanzamiento

**Caption**

Your next trip deserves a better start. We're building Cibaura: a place to explore rental cars from local agencies and individual hosts. Coming soon. Follow along as we get ready to launch.

**CTA:** Follow the launch.

**Alt:** Editorial paper illustration of a copper route, an olive destination pin and a navy key against a warm coastal-inspired background. Text announces an upcoming car-rental marketplace. Illustration, not available inventory.

## Cómo funciona

**Caption**

Find your car. Request your booking. Read and sign the rental agreement before pickup. That's the experience we're building. Bookings require host acceptance; this is a preview, not a live offer.

**CTA:** Follow for the walkthrough.

**Alt:** Three connected paper illustrations: a destination pin, a booking request card, and a key beside a rental document. The steps read Find your car, Request your booking, and Sign, then pick up. Coming soon; host acceptance required.

## Lo que diferencia la propuesta

**Caption**

Arrive with a plan. We're building around verified agencies, airport delivery options where a host offers coverage, and rental agreements signed before pickup. Delivery is not offered by every host or at every airport. Follow the launch for confirmed destinations and availability.

**CTA:** Which destination would you like to see?

**Alt:** An illustrated shield, paper airplane and contract with a pen represent agency verification, optional airport delivery and signing before pickup. Copy clearly identifies the experience as a preview and delivery as dependent on host and coverage.

## Propietarios particulares

**Caption**

Your car could be part of someone else's trip. Cibaura is being built for individual owners as well as rental agencies. Explore becoming a host and earning from eligible bookings, subject to verification and platform terms. Income is not guaranteed. Coming soon.

**CTA:** Interested in hosting? Follow for launch updates.

**Alt:** A large illustrated copper key hangs from an olive strap beside the headline Your car. A new opportunity. Text invites individual owners to explore hosting, subject to verification and platform terms, without guaranteed income.

## Secuencia sugerida

Publicar primero pre-lanzamiento; después cómo funciona, diferenciales y propietarios. No presupone un calendario de apertura ni una frecuencia prometida. El post para propietarios tiene su propia audiencia y CTA: no usarlo como si fuera un anuncio para alquilar un carro como cliente.
